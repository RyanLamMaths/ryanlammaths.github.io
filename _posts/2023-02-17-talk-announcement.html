Hi everyone! I am excited to announce that I will be giving a talk next Tuesday at the University of Arizona, Graduate Student Colloquium for Maths Students! Here are the title and the abstract of the talk. The presentation file can be found here.

Covering Surfaces and Differential Equations

What does it mean for a (complex-valued) function, such as \(\log z\), to be “multi-valued”? In answering this question, we review important concepts in Algebraic Topology such as coverings and deck transformations. If time allows, we hope to illustrate the power of considering pullbacks on the universal covering surface by discussing differential equations related to them.

Supervised by Dr Douglas Haessig.
