I talked about what I learnt in moving into a new home and trying to bake.

Since moving into my newly rented house from my student halls, I had to adapt to the new environment. Living and knowing flatmates that I haven’t seen before, reorganizing a lot of daily necessities and activities… etc. Although it was a challenging experience for me, I enjoy the process a lot. Specifically, I started to develop an interest in baking.

Our kitchen in the rented house is quite large, which means there is a lot of equipment for me to use and sharpen my cooking skills. And when at weekends where I had some spare time, I tried to find some recipes online, buy the ingredients at a store, and bake them the week after.

The first few times of baking is definitely challenging, sometimes I made a real mess and ended up spending more time cleaning than cooking! But after a few trials and errors, I started to get the hang of it. Specifically, I learnt to wash and clean different utensils as you go along and prepare ahead for the next steps in a more natural way. In the end, I would say I am quite satisfied with the result.

I think the process of baking hinted at some lessons about managing my time in university life. There are moments where you must multitask, such as waiting for the dough to rest and preparing the fillings, and it is best to plan ahead and be clear on what you are about to do! This is also true in studying: where sometimes you have to think about the tools or the resources that you will need before starting a project.

But most importantly, it is always good to experiment with different strategies and methods, just as you would in making a cake! Chances are you probably will not be satisfied with your first result. After learning this lesson, I feel more motivated in trying different things regarding my life and studies.
