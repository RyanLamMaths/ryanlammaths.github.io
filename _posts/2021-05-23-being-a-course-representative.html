My secondary school life is very academic-intensive and fruitful. However, upon reviewing my personal profile at my last year, I notice that I did not participate in a lot of competition and projects…

So the main reason is probably that there were so many people that are much stronger than me academically, so I never really got the chance. In mathematics, I am not that keen on participating in competition anyways (maybe more on that on a blog post later), and there were much fewer project opportunities compared to other applied science subject, such as Biology (which I am notoriously bad at).

…and I feel like there is a need for a change.

To be honest, upon entering the University of Bristol, (negatively speaking) I was quite desperate to convince myself that I indeed have some value in the School of Mathematics, and that’s why I have been looking for different roles that I can take and to experience something new, something that I can’t get in my Secondary school.

Consequently, one of my goals I set for myself would be improving my skills as an effective communicator. Therefore, I had nominated myself into various experience and volunteering organized by the University of Bristol Students’​ Union. This also comes with a purpose to know how to school operates as a democratic system, and how are students’ voice represented.

I attended different meetings with senior members of the staff and other course rep and discussed various feedback from other coursemates. During the process, I also learnt to collect opinions using different social media: from creating and handing out google forms in WhatsApp chat, to compiling the statistics in excel and report back to the staff, I learnt a lot while being a middle man for two paramount bodies in the University.

After this year, this kind of boosted my confidence in various aspect. It is very rewarding to see that you can make a change in the University’s decision, even very minimally. I will continue to pursue related representative (even a Faculty representative next year?), and aim to help and support all of the student in my course as fullest as possible. So if you are a student studying the same course with me reading this, remember to give me some support! Haha.
