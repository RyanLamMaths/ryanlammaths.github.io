Hi Everyone! The School of Mathematics at the University of Bristol is organizing an event to let more students know about internships in mathematics. I will be one of the student panelists!

Maths Student Internships Panel Event and Pizza Evening – an event with students for students

Monday 16 October 2023, 18:30 pm

Location: Fry LG.02 and Fry Building atrium

Hear from our experience and discover what’s in an internship or placement for you, how to get one and chat to our expert panel to find out more.

Our student panel will be able to talk and take questions about their experience of internships and year in industry placements for mathematicians and data scientists. They will represent positions taken in business and in research so you can truly get to grips with what you can achieve in your summer break or year in industry.

The tickets are sold out already, but if you want to come at 6pm, you probably would find me anyway…

I will talk about my experience in my summer research project, I might write up some findings here, or in a separate post, so let’s see how it will go!
