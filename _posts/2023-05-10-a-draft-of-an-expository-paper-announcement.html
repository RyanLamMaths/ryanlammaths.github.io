Hi everyone! I am happy to announce that I finish a draft version of an expository paper – Picard-Fuchs Equations and Cusp Forms! This will be used for part of my requirements for the MATH 492 Directed Research Unit at the University of Arizona.

I tried my best to put everything that I have learnt this semester into this paper. Although I am quite happy that I finished this, I do feel like I could do better. So, I decided to settle for a draft version of the paper, and decided that I will add details later in time – However, this is definitely wishful thinking…

This paper has the most important content all polished and good to go – in particular, the Sage code is what I am proud of the most! However, I feel like it is not very self-contained, as I feel like most topics can have better explanations.

Anyways, this also marks the end of my exchange year in Arizona! I am so glad that I met nice people: undergrads and PhD students, professors, and lecturers, that helped me become who I am today! Just want to say a big thank you to you all. And if you are reading this – thank you as well! Thank you for taking the time to read all this.

I will update more of my summer plans and what is happening next year on future blog posts.

~Ryan, 9 May 2023
