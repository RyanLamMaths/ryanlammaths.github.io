Ending my trip to Arizona in May 2023, I thought I would have a long summer holiday (school starts in late September for Bristol). However, I was wrong, somewhat gratefully, when my email inbox got a few acceptance letters.

So here is a post collecting some experiences that I did, and will be doing this academic year. They are mainly about mathematics and teaching, and I am looking forward to doing more of both in the future!

LMS Undergraduate Summer School

When I arrived in the UK, I immediately went to the University of Sheffield for the LMS Undergraduate Summer School. Which is my first time joining an undergraduate mathematics event in the UK. I met so many amazing people with different interests, and I really enjoyed the conversations: whether it was on maths, on our universities, or just chit-chatting!

In the 2 weeks of summer school, we did 6 mini-courses on different topics: such as probability, number theory, and algebraic geometry. I found out that there is a lot of algebra involved in mathematical physics (at least, in the way Dr. Alex Schenkel had taught it) and I enjoyed it a lot! We also did problem sessions, and there are various colloquiums to expose us to the frontiers of mathematics research!

I really enjoyed the time that I could intensely think about mathematics, and got in touch with different new concepts and play around with them. Especially in algebra, I learned about a categorical way to think about tensor products (as `machines’ that turn multi-linear maps into linear maps). And I learned to appreciate the categorical definition more!

Summer Research Project

After the summer school, I went back to Bristol to start my summer research project. I got an office shared with other research students! And I got a desk of my own too.

That being said, you usually would find me writing on the whiteboard in the computer room…

Anyways, the project is titled “Local Separators and Graphs”, and it is my first experience in full-time research. It is a project on combinatorics and a challenging one! I am writing more about this project, but the post is a WIP, so stay tuned!

Other Career Updates

I am now teaching more at Bristol! As some of you have noticed, there is now a “Teaching” tab on the navigation bar. Quite a lot of information is on there, so let me elaborate a little bit.

  • I am now returning as a course representative and a student advocate for Bristol Study Skills! I took on course rep in my first year, and student advocate in second. I will once again lead the Coding Club, so stay tuned for another post for more information!
  • I am now a Maths Cafe Leader! Maths cafes are optional support sessions for second-year students. As a leader, I aim to provide a space for students to talk about mathematics, and solve problems with them together. I am a leader for the course “Metric Spaces” for the first semester, and “Introduction to Geometry” next.

Since it is the start of the semester, I actually just started these roles! This is going to be a hectic year, but I will try to post updates of interesting things that I did.
