I am very happy to announce that the Coding Club, the peer-support session run by Bristol Study Skills is back up and running!

I wrote about my experience with the coding club here, I really enjoyed helping others and am looking forward to helping more students in Bristol this year! Here are the blurb and booking details of the workshop.

The Coding Club

This is a study group for students who are working on coding problems at a beginner to intermediate level. You’re welcome to come whether you’re looking for coding help or to use it as a study space to work on your coding.

This session is for you if:

  • You’re new to programming, or aren’t super confident with working on coding-based problems
  • You have upcoming deadlines for your coding problems and you’d like to come for advice or just work through the problems with the opportunity of support.
  • You’re looking for guidance on how to start coding from scratch.
  • We can offer help in the following languages: Python, Matlab, R, and general support in other programming languages.

This is a safe space to make mistakes, ask silly questions, and develop both your problem-solving skills and your confidence. Bring along any programming problems that you’re working on at the moment.

Coding Club takes place every Wednesday during term time 15:00 – 17:00 in Room 1.14 in the Physics Building. Book Now: https://bristol.ac.uk/students/your-studies/study-support/study-skills/workshops/#tags=677585220
