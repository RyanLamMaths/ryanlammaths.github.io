While planning for university options in Grade 11 (Form 5 in HK), although I had a clear idea of pursuing mathematics, I never actually got a taste of what university (studying) life is. With curiosity and no knowledge of any topics whatsoever, I stumbled into this course. Little did I know it will become one of the most influential mathematical events to me.

Two courses are offered to me during this programme: “Understanding Non-Euclidean Geometry” in July, and “Number Theory and Cryptography” in August. Both courses took place at the campus of CUHK, with lectures (09:30-12:00), and tutorials (13:00-16:15) for 10 days, using only 3 weeks per course. At the end of each week, there would be a test, with a final exam at the end of the whole course. Grades and scholarships will be awarded according to them.

No need to say, this was a very high-intensity training for me, especially during the non-Euclidean Geometry part, where knowledge about different proofing techniques and mathematical object is already assumed. I had to extract information from the lecture using the 1-hour lunchtime and apply it during our tutorial, where the teaching assistants would demonstrate follow-up question and assign different homework that prepared us for the quiz.

Nevertheless, by absorbing all the content rapidly, I realized I enjoyed the two courses a lot. From Mobius transformation to models of hyperbolic geometry, from the Chinese remainder theorem to the RSA encryption algorithm, the course acted as a huge expository talk for different branches of mathematics. The teaching assistant’s comment and other guest lectures in the program also helped me a lot in consolidating and understanding mathematics in general.

As a result, I am awarded the “Excellence Performance Award” in the course at the Number Theory course in August, and completed the course with a scholarship. It enormously boosted my confidence in university mathematics.

~ Ryan, 21 May 2021
