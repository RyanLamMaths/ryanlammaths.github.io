Hi everyone! Despite a few drafts in my notebook which I am still procrastinating to finish, here are some life updates!

Graduated!

I have graduated from the University of Bristol, achieving a First Class Honours in MSci Mathematics with Study Abroad!

I want to thank everyone for the support, fun times and laughter in 4 years of my undergraduate life! I enjoyed it very much.

Next Steps

I am also thrilled to announce that my journey with mathematics will not stop here. I will continue to do a PhD in Mathematics here in Bristol, supervised by Professor Jeremy Rickard!

I am very grateful that I can continue my journey with mathematics! I hope to discover more, learn more, and tell others about more interesting facts in mathematics. Please look forward to more presentations, writings and updates that will be posted on this website!

Some Stuff in Summer

While I was on my summer holiday back in Hong Kong from June to August, I also got the chance to teach the Enrichment Programme for Young Mathematics Talents as a teaching assistant. This is a course for talented high school students, hoping they get some hands-on experience with university mathematics. (See the poster attached here.)

I was a TA for the “Towards Differential Geometry” course. During the entire August, I proposed questions for the quizzes and exams, prepared tutorial notes, and taught two 3-hour tutorial sessions. It was a fun experience, and I made a lot of connections with mathematicians and mathematicians-to-be!

And thanks to the enthusiasm of the students, I could be sneaky and talk about some out-of-syllabus materials…! Go to my LinkedIn Profile and see what it is!

What’s next?

I am currently settling down as a PhD Student, joining conferences such as the Heilbronn Annual Conference, and filling out forms to make sure everything is “officially okay”. Oh also, I have an office space now! This is going to be exciting…
