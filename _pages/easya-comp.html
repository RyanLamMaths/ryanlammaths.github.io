Aim

This competition is inspired by Team Mathematics Challenge, hosted by the UK Mathematics Trust. We want to give different students the opportunity to tackle math questions and have a taste of what university mathematics is like while developing teamwork and communication skills.

Prizes

There will be a £40 Amazon Voucher split across the teammates for the winning team of the competition! You will also receive a participation certificate to show your team’s collaborative and communication skills!

Eligibility

The EasyA Team Maths Challenge is designed for all students studying mathematics from secondary school, i.e. from year 7 to 13! You can form a team of up to (and including) 4 students.

How to Enter

Please fill in the google form here to register your interest! The deadline had passed! But there will be more competitions coming!

Format of the Competition

Teams are given a paper that contains 3 sets of questions:

  • Part A: GCSE Level Questions, 10 questions, 5 marks each,
  • Part B: Harder GCSE / AS Level Mathematics Questions, 5 Questions, 10 Marks Each
  • Part C: Difficult AS / A Level Mathematics Questions, 4 Questions, 15 Marks Each.

IMPORTANT: YEAR 13 STUDENTS ARE NOT ALLOWED TO HELP YOUR TEAMMATES ON PART A QUESTIONS TO ENSURE FAIRNESS

And their aim is to divide up among themselves to answer the questions and get as many points as possible.
